About the museum

What is teamLab Borderless?

A museum of interactive digital art where art, technology and creativity come together in an immersive experience.

✦ Image placeholder assets/img/about-museum.svg
First permanent museum of its kind
in the Middle East

Located in Culture Square in Historic Jeddah, it is the first permanent museum of its kind in the Middle East. It spans nearly 10,000 square metres and houses more than 80 artworks that interact with one another and with visitors.

“

Art is not just a painting we look at — it is an experience we live and take part in, where every movement and discovery can lead to a new scene.

The idea behind the museum

Here, artworks move and change before your eyes, travelling from one space to another with no fixed route. You are not a spectator; your presence and movement are part of the artwork itself.
